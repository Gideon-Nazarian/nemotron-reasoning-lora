### B. Logprob-filtering — FEASIBLE, ~1-2h scoring pass (agent 2)
- Tong's actual filter = training-time per-epoch min-logprob triage (threshold −0.69 ≈ ln 0.5) that SURFACES
  high-loss problems for a HUMAN to regenerate; shipped corpus is all `included:true`. The doc's "automated
  teacher-forced filter" is a valid ADAPTATION (automating his manual loop), not literally his code.
- For US it's actually MORE appropriate: our traces are externally authored (dgxchen/ours) → we have a pool to
  score-and-rank. Procedure: load base 30B, ONE teacher-forced forward per trace (no generation), mean/min logprob
  over assistant tokens, drop low ones (per-category top-X% > hard global threshold).
- Cost: ~30min-2h for ~10K traces; ~1000× cheaper than generation (single forward vs thousands of decode steps);
  fits the 12h cap. **CORRECTION to agent's VRAM worry:** our Kaggle RTX Pro 6000 is 96GB (v18 loads 30B bf16 at
  ~85GB peak) → bf16 scoring FITS; no 4-bit needed. Add as a cell in v18 before training.
- generation.jsonl shortcut = DEAD (base-model GENERATION logprobs over HIS traces/problems; not joinable, not
  teacher-forced over our text). Reuse only the method + the ln(0.5) anchor.
